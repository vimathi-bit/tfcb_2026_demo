# Experiment: PCR amplify gBlock DNA fragment for HDR template 

Date: 2026-10-11

Issue: [Issue #1](https://github.com/vimathi-bit/tfcb_2026_demo/issues/1)

## Reagents (for PCR)

| reagent | vendor | catalog number | stock concentration | final concentration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gBlock template DNA | IDT | N/A | 10ng/uL | 0.2ng/uL |
| Forward/reverse primers | IDT | N/A | 10uM each | 0.5uM each|
| dNTP | NEB | N0446S | 10mM | 200uM |
| Phusion high-fidelity (HF) DNA polymerase | Thermo | F530S | 2U/ul | 0.04U/ul |
| 5X Phusion HF buffer | NEB | B0518S | 5X | 1X |
| Nuclease-free water | N/A | N/A | N/A| N/A |

## Procedure

1. Thaw all reagents on ice. 
2. Assemble a 50uL PCR reaction containing 1uL gBlock template DNA, 2.5uL of each primer, 1uL dNTP, 1uL Phusion HF DNA polymerase, 10uL 5X Phusion HF buffer, and 32uL nuclease-free water.
3. Place the tubes in the thermocycler.
4. Run initial denaturation at 98°C for 30s.
5. Run 20 cycles of denaturation at 98°C for 10s, annealing at 60°C for 30s, and extension at 72°C for 20s.
6. Run final extension at 72°C for 2min, then hold at 4 °C.
7. Run 5ul of product on a 2% agarose gel at 120V for 40 min.
8. Purify the product with a PCR clean-up and elute in 10uL.
9. Measure concentration on a NanoDrop.
